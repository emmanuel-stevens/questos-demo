Privacy Policy

QuestOS — last updated 14 August 2026

QuestOS is a personal planning tool. It turns your own projects into quests you can see. This page describes exactly what it stores, what leaves your device, and what it never touches.

QuestOS is currently a private beta run by one person. There is no advertising, no tracking pixels, no advertising or cross-site trackers, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing. There is no business model here that depends on knowing things about you. The public tutorial counts three anonymous events so its author can tell whether anyone reached the end — described in full below.

Who runs this

QuestOS is operated by Emmanuel Stevens as an individual. For any privacy question, or to ask for your data or its deletion, write to hello@questos.io.

What is stored, and where

WhatWhere it livesWhy
Your realm — quests, tasks, notes, journal entries, progress In your browser, and (if you sign in) in a row of our database that only your account can read It is your planner. Without it there is nothing to show you.
Your email address Our authentication provider (Supabase) Sign-in is a one-time emailed link. There are no passwords, so there is no password to store or leak.

Google Drive — what we can and cannot see

Connecting Drive is optional and nothing depends on it.

  • QuestOS requests only the drive.file scope. Google grants this app access only to the specific files you choose in Google's own file picker — never your folders, never the rest of your Drive, and never anything you create later.
  • QuestOS stores a link, never a copy. It does not download, read, index, or analyse the contents of your files.
  • Because no content is read, nothing from your Drive is ever sent to an AI model.
  • The access token Google issues is held in memory for the length of one file selection and then discarded. It is never written to disk or to our database.
  • You can disconnect QuestOS at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing a link inside QuestOS does not touch the file in your Drive.

QuestOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Calendar

If you supply a read-only calendar link, QuestOS reads busy times only to suggest open hours. This access is read-only by construction: QuestOS has no ability to create, change, or delete a calendar event, and it never sends invitations. Suggested times are copied by you, by hand, into your own calendar app.

AI features, and what reaches a model

QuestOS can answer questions about your plan using an AI model provided by Anthropic. When you ask, a short summary is sent: your task titles, dates, effort levels and counts, plus what you typed.

  • Your journal and diary entries are never sent unless you paste them into a message yourself.
  • Captured notes are sent only for the one item you explicitly ask about.
  • Model providers process this to answer and, per their terms, do not use it to train their models.
  • If you connect your own AI subscription instead, these requests leave our servers entirely and run under your own account and terms.

The public tutorial counts three things

The tutorial at emmanuel-stevens.github.io/questos-demo is a free walkthrough with a real goal behind it: its author needs to know whether people who open it ever reach the end. So it counts three events, and only these three:

EventSent when
arrivedThe tutorial is opened.
induction_startedYou complete your first task in it.
graduatedYou finish it and dismiss the closing letter.
  • The payload is the event name and nothing else. No task titles, no text you wrote, no realm data, no identifier of any kind. The three words above are literally all that is sent.
  • The counter is GoatCounter: no cookies, no visitor profiles, no cross-site tracking, open source. It was chosen to match this policy rather than the other way round.
  • Nothing you type in the tutorial ever leaves your browser — the tutorial realm lives in local storage on your own device.
  • The last two events are counted once per browser, so this cannot be used to follow a returning visitor around.
  • This applies to the public tutorial only. It is not present in a signed-in realm’s own planning, and the counter is absent entirely from builds made without it configured.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers — ever.
  • No tracking cookies, no fingerprinting, and no third-party advertising or analytics scripts beyond the three cookieless tutorial events described above.
  • No reading of your files, your inbox, or your other accounts.
  • No profile built about you, and no attempt to identify you across other sites.

Who else touches your data

Only the services required to run the software: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Anthropic (AI responses, when you use them), Google (only if you connect Drive), and GoatCounter (the three tutorial event counts above, and nothing else). Each holds only what its job requires.

How long it is kept, and leaving

Your realm is kept while your account exists. Leaving is real leaving: ask us to delete your account and your realm row is deleted, not archived or anonymised and retained. You can export everything you have at any time from inside the app.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to hello@questos.io. QuestOS is not intended for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your realm, beta users will be told directly rather than by a silent edit to this page.
